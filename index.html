Hello Everyone! Welcome to the annual Wizardry Competition

How to Play

You are a wizard locked in a duel with a rival sorcerer. Cast spells by solving math problems before the timer runs out!

Stats

  • Both wizards start with 100 HP.
  • You start with 60 mana.

Casting a Spell

  1. Click Cast. This costs 5 mana, and mana is never refunded.
  2. A window opens with 10 seconds on the clock.
  3. Solve 3 math problems, one at a time (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division).
  4. If time runs out, your spell fizzles, even if you answered some correctly.

Magic Frenzy

Cast 3 perfect spells in a row (all 3 answers correct each time) to trigger Magic Frenzy. Your next spell deals +5 bonus damage. Miss even one answer and your streak resets. The bonus does not help a fizzled spell.

The Rival Wizard

Every round, the rival hits you for 10 damage, no matter what happens.

Winning and Losing

  • Win: bring the rival to 0 HP.
  • Lose: your HP reaches 0, or you run out of mana.
  • Draw: you both reach 0 HP in the same round. There is no winner.
